The app opens on the Home Space, both for guests and for signed-in users; the
Sign In and Sign Up screens are reached from the first launch or from
Settings. The Home Space is the hub: its four objects open the services, and
the bottom bar opens History and Profile. The Session Summary is not opened
from a menu; the app shows it automatically when a break ends.
Figure~\ref{fig:sitemap} shows the screens and Table~\ref{tab:routes} lists
their routes.
