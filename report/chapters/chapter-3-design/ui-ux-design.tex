\subsection{Design Principles and Visual Language}
\label{sec:design-principles}

The user interface of Breakly is founded upon the design philosophy of \textit{Calm Computing} and \textit{Mindful Digital Well-being}. Unlike conventional media applications that leverage endless feeds, algorithmic recommendations, and variable-reward notifications to maximize user screen time, Breakly is engineered around explicit boundaries, predictable stopping points, and minimal cognitive friction. The visual design establishes a restful virtual environment where users can decompress without falling into digital consumption rabbit holes.

\subsubsection{Dual-Theme Atmosphere Architecture}
Breakly delivers two distinct sensory atmospheres tailored to ambient lighting conditions and psychological resting contexts:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Light Mode (Daytime / Garden Room)}: Utilizes soft linen and warm parchment backgrounds (\texttt{\#F4EDE1}, \texttt{\#FAF6F0}), grounded by muted slate-gray typography (\texttt{\#2D3139}) and a calming deep emerald green accent (\texttt{\#2F6B5E}). This aesthetic evokes an airy sunlit sunroom, ideal for daytime study and work pauses.
  \item \textbf{Dark Mode (Nighttime / Cozy Chill Room)}: Employs a nocturnal palette centered on deep slate-navy canvas tones (\texttt{\#1B1C29}, \texttt{\#232437}), accented with warm ambient amber glow (\texttt{\#E8B97C}) and soothing pastel night tones. The atmosphere emulates a dimly lit study at night, eliminating blue-light glare and eye strain.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Typography, Elevation, and Component Tokens}
The typography system relies on the platform native sans-serif font family (\textit{Inter} / \textit{SF Pro} on iOS, \textit{Roboto} on Android) to ensure instant rendering with zero layout shift. Fluid typographic hierarchy is enforced across three primary tiers:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Display \& Headlines}: 22--28\,pt (Semi-Bold / Bold), line height 1.25, applied to room greeting banners, time indicators, and modal headlines.
  \item \textbf{Body Text}: 14--16\,pt (Regular / Medium), line height 1.45, used for news reader paragraphs, onboarding descriptions, and track metadata.
  \item \textbf{Captions \& Metadata}: 11--12\,pt (Medium), uppercase tracking $+0.5$\,pt, applied to countdown badges, station genres, and timestamps.
\end{itemize}
All interactive surfaces employ gentle elevation with soft multi-layered shadows ($Y=2\,\text{dp}$, blur $=8\,\text{dp}$, opacity $=0.08$) and generous border radii ($16\,\text{dp}$ to $24\,\text{dp}$ on surface cards; fully pill-shaped $999\,\text{dp}$ for countdown badges and action buttons).

\subsection{User Flows and Interaction Architecture}
\label{sec:user-flows}

The application interaction architecture is organized into three interlocking loops: the \textit{Onboarding Loop}, the \textit{Primary Break Loop}, and the \textit{Reflection Loop}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Onboarding \& On-Ramp Flow}: First-time users are greeted by a brand philosophy splash, followed by a 3-step value carousel introducing time-boxing, virtual room hotspots, and automatic tear-down. Users then authenticate (or proceed as guest) to preserve streaks and preferences.
  \item \textbf{Primary Micro-Break Session Flow}:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt]
      \item \textit{Pre-Commitment}: From the virtual room, the user selects a strict session quota ($10$, $20$, or $30$ minutes) \textit{before} tapping any content item.
      \item \textit{Ambient Immersion}: The user taps an in-room hotspot: the \textbf{Radio} (audio streaming), the \textbf{Newspaper} (clean RSS news reader), or the \textbf{TV} (curated short video player).
      \item \textit{Media Arbitration}: Audio playback runs concurrently while the user reads articles. When the TV is turned on, background music automatically suspends to prevent competing audio streams.
      \item \textit{Non-Intrusive Countdown Warning}: At $1$ minute remaining, an ambient toast badge gently fades in, allowing the user to wind down their attention without sudden panic.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Hard Tear-Down \& Reflection Flow}: At exactly $00:00$, all media players and WebViews are unmounted immediately. A calming \textit{Time's Up} quote transitions to a concise \textit{Break Summary} metrics card, recording the session to the local SQLite database and remote backend.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Visual and Accessibility Guidelines Compliance}
\label{sec:visual-accessibility}

Every high-fidelity screen has been audited against the \textbf{W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 Level AA/AAA)} and mobile platform ergonomics standards:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Color Contrast Ratios}:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=1pt]
      \item \textit{Light Theme}: Dark slate body text (\texttt{\#2D3139}) against warm linen background (\texttt{\#F4EDE1}) yields a contrast ratio of $10.4:1$, vastly exceeding the WCAG AAA threshold of $7.0:1$.
      \item \textit{Dark Theme}: Light ivory text (\texttt{\#E2E8F0}) against dark slate canvas (\texttt{\#1B1C29}) achieves a contrast ratio of $12.8:1$.
      \item \textit{Accent Controls}: Action buttons (\texttt{\#2F6B5E} background with white glyphs) achieve $4.85:1$, satisfying the WCAG AA minimum for normal interactive text.
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Touch Target Sizing}: All interactive pills, radio cards, navigation tabs, and media controls maintain a bounding hit area of at least $44 \times 44\,\text{dp}$ on iOS and $48 \times 48\,\text{dp}$ on Android, surrounded by a minimum $8\,\text{dp}$ spacing buffer to prevent accidental taps.
  \item \textbf{Dynamic Font Scaling}: Layouts are architected using React Native flexbox containers with responsive wrap and auto-scrolling fallback, guaranteeing that system font enlargement up to $200\%$ does not cause text truncation or container overflow.
  \item \textbf{Reduced Motion}: In compliance with platform accessibility settings (\texttt{prefers-reduced-motion}), the app disables particle floating animations and transitions instantly between screens, replacing scale/fade effects with immediate opacity rendering.
  \item \textbf{Screen Reader Semantics}: Every visual hotspot (Radio, Newspaper, TV) includes explicit accessibility labels, hints (e.g., ``Radio hotspot, double tap to listen to ambient music''), and state announcements (e.g., ``Break in progress, 18 minutes remaining'').
\end{enumerate}
