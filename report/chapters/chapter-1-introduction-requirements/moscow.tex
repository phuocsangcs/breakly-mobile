\subsubsection{MoSCoW Analysis}

Table~\ref{tab:moscow} summarises the prioritisation of the functional
requirements using the MoSCoW scheme. Must and Should requirements correspond
to the priorities given in Table~\ref{tab:functional-requirements}; all of them
are implemented in the current build. Could and Won't items are listed so that
the boundary of the project is explicit; Could items have no requirement
identifiers because they are not specified for this version.
