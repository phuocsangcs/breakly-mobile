\subsubsection{MosCow Analysis}

Table~\ref{tab:moscow} summarises the prioritisation of the functional
requirements using the MoSCoW scheme. Must and Should requirements correspond
to the priorities given in Table~\ref{tab:functional-requirements}. Could and
Won't items are listed so that the boundary of the project is explicit; Could
items have no requirement identifiers because they will be specified only if
time allows.
