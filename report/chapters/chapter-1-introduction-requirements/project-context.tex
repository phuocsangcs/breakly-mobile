\section{Project Context}
\label{sec:problem-statement}

Breakly is a mobile application for helping people take intentional,
time-boxed micro-breaks instead of continuing unstructured screen use. The
problem addressed by this project is the difficulty of starting, following,
and ending a short restorative break during study or work. Users need a
simple way to pause, choose an activity, and return to their task without
being overwhelmed by another complex productivity tool.

\subsection{Domain Context}
\label{sec:domain-context}

Breakly addresses the need for intentional, time-boxed micro-breaks during
study and work. The domain combines personal productivity, wellness-oriented
break activities, reminders, and mobile interaction design without making
medical or clinical claims.

\subsection{Related Apps}
\label{sec:related-apps}

The project will compare Breakly with timer, habit, mindfulness, and
productivity applications. The comparison focuses on onboarding, break
duration controls, activity suggestions, notifications, history, offline
behaviour, and accessibility.

\subsection{System Overview}
\label{sec:system-overview}

Breakly is a mobile-first application containing a break-session engine,
activity catalogue, preferences, local history, and optional notification and
remote synchronisation services. The core break flow remains usable offline.

\subsection{Personas}
\label{sec:personas}

\subsubsection{Linh -- The Overloaded Student}
Linh is a university student who studies in long sessions and often ignores
breaks until she feels mentally exhausted. She needs a one-tap break flow,
short activity suggestions, and a reminder that does not interrupt her work.

\subsubsection{Minh -- The Desk-Based Professional}
Minh works at a computer and wants a predictable five-to-ten-minute reset
between meetings. He values configurable duration, a calm interface, and
the ability to resume or skip an activity without losing control.

\subsubsection{An -- The Accessibility-Conscious User}
An uses larger text and prefers high-contrast interfaces. An needs readable
content, sufficiently large touch targets, and timer feedback that is not
communicated by colour alone.
