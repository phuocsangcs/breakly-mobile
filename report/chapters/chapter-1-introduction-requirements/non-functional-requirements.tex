\subsubsection{Non-functional requirements}

The non-functional requirements of Breakly set measurable targets for how well
the app must perform on a phone. Normal conditions means a Wi-Fi or 4G connection of at least 5 Mbps. The reference devices are one Android phone and
one iPhone (iPhone 15, iOS 16).

NFR-01 & Response time & Under normal conditions, the Home Space, News, Radio
and TV screens show content or a loading state within 3 seconds, and taps give
visible feedback within 100 ms; verify with the median of 10 runs on each
reference device. \\
\hline

NFR-02 & Background behaviour & The countdown stays within $\pm$ 2 seconds of
real time after the app is backgrounded or the device is locked, and music
keeps playing with the screen locked until the session ends; verify with a
3-minute background test and a 30-minute locked-screen session. \\
\hline

NFR-03 & Offline behaviour & When the connection is lost, an offline message
with a retry option appears within 10 seconds, the countdown continues, and
unsaved session records are uploaded after the connection returns; verify with
airplane mode during a session. \\
\hline

NFR-04 & Security & Sign-in tokens are kept in secure device storage, all
traffic uses HTTPS, a user can access only their own records, and no secret key
is committed to the repository; verify by inspecting the app storage, by
trying to read a second test account's records (must be denied), and by a
secret scan of the Git history. \\
\hline

NFR-05 & Permissions & The app requests no location, contacts, camera or
microphone permission, and any notification permission is requested only in
context, when the user turns on a reminder; verify by inspecting the declared
permissions and by running all Must flows with notifications denied. \\
\hline

NFR-06 & Platform and OS versions & The app runs on Android 8.0 or later and iOS
16 or later \textbf{[TODO: confirm against the chosen Expo SDK]}, in portrait,
on screen widths from 360 to 430 dp; verify on the reference devices and on an
emulator of the lowest supported version. \\
\hline

NFR-07 & Battery and network & A 20-minute session with music playing and the
screen on uses at most 8 percent of the battery (initial target), and music is
streamed at no more than 128 kbps; verify with the device battery and network
statistics. \\
\hline

NFR-08 & Accessibility & Core screens support text scaling up to 130 percent,
touch targets of at least 48 $\times$ 48 dp, text contrast of at least 4.5:1,
and status that is never shown by colour alone; verify with an accessibility
scanner and a manual test at the larger text size. \\
\hline
