\chapter{Demonstration}
\label{chap:demonstration}

The demonstration uses the development build on an iPhone, connected to the
backend on the same Wi-Fi network. Each flow lists the steps and the
requirements it shows.

\section{Basic Flow: A Complete Break}
\label{sec:basic-flow}

This flow shows the core promise of Breakly: an enjoyable break with a clear
end (UC-04, UC-07, UC-09).
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Open the app and select \textit{Continue as guest}. The Home Space
        shows the Cozy chill room with the wall clock at the current time and
        clouds drifting past the window.
  \item Select 10 minutes and tap the radio. The countdown starts and the
        Radio screen opens.
  \item Choose the Jazz station. The track starts with its artwork and
        licence; drag the seek bar and change the volume. Back on the Home
        Space, music notes float above the radio and the mini-player is shown.
  \item Lock the phone: the music continues and the lock screen shows the
        track. Unlock, open the newspaper and read an article in the in-app
        reader while the timer keeps running.
  \item At one minute left, the ``One minute left'' toast appears. At 00:00
        the music stops, the reader closes, and the Time's Up screen and the
        Session Summary show the time used, the music time and one article
        opened.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Break Configuration Flow}
\label{sec:break-configuration-flow}

This flow shows that the user's settings shape the next break and are kept
(UC-01, UC-06, UC-14, UC-13).
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Open Profile, select \textit{Sign in / Create account} and create an
        account. The guest break from the basic flow is uploaded to the new
        account.
  \item Change the default break length to 20 minutes, turn the one-minute
        warning off and set the radio volume to 60 percent.
  \item Switch the theme to Normal garden with the switch in the Home Space
        header: the room, the sky (now sunny) and the colours change while the
        objects stay in place.
  \item Close and reopen the app: the account, the theme and the settings are
        kept, and the duration selector shows 20 minutes.
  \item Start a break and end it early with \textit{End break}. The summary
        appears, and History lists both breaks with the current streak.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Additional Flow: Videos, Games and Offline Music}
\label{sec:additional-flow}

This flow shows the features that keep a break finite and the offline
behaviour (UC-08, UC-11, UC-12).
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item Start a break by tapping the TV. The music pauses and the mobile
        YouTube website opens inside the app. Open several videos; the counter
        shows ``Video $n$ of 10''. After the tenth video the end card appears;
        back on the Home Space the music resumes.
  \item Tap the gamepad, open Wordle, play and go back. Wordle is marked as
        played today and its streak increases; opening it again does not
        record a second play.
  \item On the Radio, download two tracks. Turn on airplane mode and play the
        \textit{Downloaded} station: the tracks play without a connection.
        Save one track to the Files app with \textit{Save to phone}.
  \item End the break: the summary counts the videos watched and the game
        played.
\end{enumerate}
